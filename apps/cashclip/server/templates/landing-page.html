The minimum withdrawal is $5.00. We incentivize higher withdrawals with cashout bonuses: $10 (+3%), $25 (+5%), $50 (+8%), $100 (+12%), and $250 (+15%). Saving up can earn you significantly more.

Higher withdrawal thresholds help us manage transaction costs and reinvest revenue for growth. By keeping funds in the ecosystem longer, we can allocate more to user payouts and sustain the platform. The bonus is our way of sharing those savings with you.
