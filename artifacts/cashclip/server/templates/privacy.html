CashClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CashClip ("CashClip", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the CashClip mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Username and password (passwords are stored only as a salted bcrypt hash).
  • Account role and account creation timestamp.
  • PayPal email address you provide for cash withdrawals.

Earnings and activity data

  • Your withdrawable balance and lifetime earnings.
  • An earnings ledger of credits and debits, including the source provider and reference IDs.
  • Reward session metadata (session token, type, provider, estimated reward, status).
  • Withdrawal requests and their status.

Advertising and device data

  • We use Google AdMob to serve rewarded video and other ad formats. AdMob may collect a mobile advertising identifier (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID), coarse device information, IP address, and ad interaction events for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • We record ad analytics events (impressions, completions, rewards earned, load failures, errors, duration, platform) on our servers to monitor app health and reward integrity. These events are linked to your CashClip account.

Survey and offerwall provider data

  • When you start a survey or offer through providers such as Pollfish, Cint, Lucid, CPX Research, BitLabs, Tapjoy, AdGate, or OfferToro, we share a pseudonymous user ID and reward-session token with that provider so completions can be credited to your account.
  • Providers may collect information directly from you in their flows (for example, demographic answers, screening responses, device or network signals used for fraud prevention). Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Providers send completion postbacks to our servers identifying the session, payout amount, and a transaction ID, which we use to credit your balance.

PayPal payout data

  • When you request a withdrawal, we transmit the requested amount and your PayPal email address to PayPal via the PayPal Payouts API in order to send the funds. PayPal returns a payout status which we store with the withdrawal record.
  • We do not collect or store your PayPal password, bank account, or card details.

Support and lead data

  • If you contact us through the support page or a related contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit so we can respond.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To track your earnings, transactions, and withdrawals accurately.
  • To deliver and credit surveys, offers, games, and rewarded ads.
  • To process PayPal cashouts you request.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate accounts, and reward gaming.
  • To analyze ad performance and improve app reliability.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How Information Is Shared

  • PayPal — your PayPal email and the payout amount are shared to send your withdrawal.
  • Survey and offerwall providers (Pollfish, Cint, Lucid, CPX Research, BitLabs, Tapjoy, AdGate, OfferToro) — receive a pseudonymous user ID and session/transaction identifiers needed to attribute rewards.
  • Google AdMob — receives device and advertising identifiers and ad interaction signals as required to serve and measure ads.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — process data on our behalf to operate the Service (for example, our hosting provider and PostgreSQL database provider).
  • Legal and safety — when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CashClip, our users, or others.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We retain account, earnings, and withdrawal records for as long as your account is active and as needed to comply with tax, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Ad analytics events and reward-session records are retained for operational and fraud-prevention purposes. You may request deletion of your account as described below.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Advertising ID — you can reset or limit your mobile advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.
  • Permissions — you can revoke optional permissions in your device's settings.
  • Account deletion — you can request deletion of your CashClip account and associated personal data by emailing support@absustaininvest.com from the email address associated with your account, or by using the in-app account deletion option where available. We may retain limited records required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, port, or delete your personal data. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

6. Children

CashClip is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including TLS encryption in transit, bcrypt password hashing, JWT-based authentication, and access controls on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International Users

CashClip is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from another country, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be announced in-app or by another reasonable means.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email support@absustaininvest.com or visit our support page.
